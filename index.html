The Dogs On Mold Way
Our four-part method

Four disciplines, one honest answer.

Most inspectors do one thing. We layer four — because mold doesn't show up in just one way, and you deserve to know it everywhere it lives.
Discipline 01

Mold Literate

We know what mold is, how it grows, and — critically — how to read the difference between an old issue and an active one. That distinction changes everything about your next steps.

  • Old vs. new water damageReading staining patterns, growth maturity, and moisture signatures to tell you whether you're looking at history or an active problem.
  • Species behaviorKnowing which molds favor which conditions — and what that tells us about the source.
Discipline 02

Building Science Literate

Mold is a symptom. Buildings are the cause. We read pressure dynamics, envelope failures, HVAC behavior, and moisture pathways — so when our dogs alert, we can explain why.

  • Top-tier building science credentialsTrained beyond detection — we understand the systems creating the problem.
  • Root cause framingEvery alert gets context: what's leaking, what's drying poorly, what the building is doing wrong.
Discipline 03

Persistence of Discovery

We don't stop at a sniff. We confirm what the dog finds with a full multi-modal toolkit — so you walk away with evidence, not a hunch.

  • Smell — the dogElite K9 training (Ironheart-trained, 35+ years working-dog lineage) with full handler certification.
  • Trained on the volatile organic compounds (VOCs) mold produces — not just visible growth.
  • Visual confirmationHigh-intensity inspection light, borescope for inside walls and out-of-sight cavities, and thermal-aware moisture metering.
  • Borescope · High-intensity light · Calibrated moisture meter
Discipline 04

Start-to-Finish Consultancy

Finding mold is step one. We stay with you through everything that comes after — because most people get a report and then get lost. Not on our watch.

  • Hygienist advocacyWe connect you with vetted industrial hygienists and translate their findings into plain English.
  • Remediation "GC"We act as your general contractor through the remediation process — qualifying companies and protecting you from upsells.
  • Legal advocacyDocumentation that holds up — whether you're negotiating with a seller, a landlord, or in court.

Why this matters for your wallet and your health

A water stain on a ceiling could be a fixed leak from five years ago — or a slow-drip disaster happening right now. Air samples can't tell you which. A visual inspector might guess. A canine inspection backed by building science gives you the actual answer.

Knowing the difference can save you tens of thousands in unnecessary remediation — or save your health by catching an active problem the last inspector missed.
