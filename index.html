Why a dog finds what people miss

Air samples miss it. Visual inspections miss it. Our dogs don't.

01

Scent over sight

Mold releases microbial gases (MVOCs) as it grows. Winnie is trained to detect those gases at trace concentrations and trace them back to the source — even when there's no obvious sign of mold growth on a reachable surface, like when it's behind a wall, under flooring, or inside the HVAC. Air sampling, by contrast, is prone to false positives and struggles to capture accurate results unless the sample is pulled in the exact vicinity of the growth.

02

Non-Invasive

No drilling, no demolition, no tearing into walls just to look. The dog finds the source first. Then a targeted plan follows.

03

Speed

A typical inspection runs 60–90 minutes — versus days of air sampling, lab waits, and inconclusive reports.

04

No repair upsell

We don't do the remediation ourselves, so we're not selling you a repair. Our job is honest detection and pointing you to the right people next.

The “Dogs On Mold” Way
How we work

Four disciplines, one honest answer.

Most inspectors do one thing. We layer four — because mold doesn't show up in just one way, and you deserve to know it everywhere it lives.
01

We read the mold, not just the dog

A dog finds the scent — but someone has to read what it means. As your handler, I'm trained to interpret the mold itself: how old it is, whether it's active, and what's feeding it. Reading staining patterns, growth maturity, and moisture signatures alongside Winnie's alerts is what turns a "she smelled something" into an actual answer about what you're dealing with.

02

We find what the dog alone would miss

Most mold-dog companies can read their dog. Few can read the building. Mold is a symptom; the structure is the cause. Because I'm trained in building science — pressure dynamics, envelope failures, HVAC behavior, moisture pathways — I can account for the spaces a dog might miss, sharpen how I read Winnie's cues in a given area, and explain why the mold is there. That's the difference between finding mold and understanding it.

03

Proof, not a hunch

We don't stop at a sniff. We confirm what Winnie finds with high-intensity inspection light and calibrated moisture metering — and, at your request, optional air or swab sampling — so you walk away with evidence, not a guess.

04

We don't disappear after the report

Finding mold is step one. We stay with you through what comes after — connecting you with vetted hygienists, and acting as your GC through remediation to protect you from bad remediators, because we know what to look for.
