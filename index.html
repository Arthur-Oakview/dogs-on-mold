Does any of this sound familiar?

The symptoms people report — long before they find the mold.

When the fog won't lift and your sharpness feels gone.

  • Brain fog
  • Headaches & migraines
  • Memory lapses & forgetfulness
  • Trouble concentrating
  • Difficulty finding words
  • Dizziness or vertigo
  • Numbness & tingling
  • Sensitivity to light & sound
  • Anxiety or low mood
  • Trouble sleeping
  • …and more

The cough, congestion, and "colds" that never fully clear.

  • Persistent cough
  • Wheezing or shortness of breath
  • Chronic congestion
  • Sinus pressure & postnasal drip
  • Frequent sinus infections
  • Sore or scratchy throat
  • New or worsening asthma
  • Itchy, watery eyes
  • Sneezing
  • Worse symptoms indoors
  • …and more

The exhaustion and "off" feeling that rest doesn't fix.

  • Deep, unshakable fatigue
  • Feel better when you leave home
  • Unrefreshing sleep
  • Mood swings & irritability
  • Getting sick often
  • Night sweats & temperature swings
  • Increased thirst
  • Appetite or weight changes
  • Static "shocks"
  • Metallic taste
  • …and more

Aches that get written off as "just getting older."

  • Joint pain & stiffness
  • Muscle aches
  • Morning stiffness
  • Muscle cramps or spasms
  • Muscle weakness
  • General inflammation
  • Reduced range of motion
  • Joint swelling
  • Persistent low-grade pain
  • …and more

Symptoms like these have many possible causes. But if yours ease when you're away from home and return when you're back, a hidden mold source is worth ruling out.

Dogs On Mold provides canine mold detection — not medical diagnosis or treatment. A few years ago, I was the one who was sick.

I lived it. That's why I built this.

It started as a migraine that wouldn't quit. Then came more symptoms, and more, until they were debilitating. Many providers and even urgent care visits — and no one could find anything wrong from standard examination or blood tests.

A friend brought up potential long-term mold exposure from my home. Elevated levels were found — the problem wasn't in my head, it was in my home.

Over three years, it cost me more than $50,000 — testing, remediation, and clawing my health back. I went on leave from work. I moved out. I went through remediation, and turned to functional health to start rebuilding what the mold had taken. Recovery was slow. But piece by piece, I got my life back.

I'm still healing — and I'm honest about that. What I'm not is in the dark anymore. That experience turned me into an advocate against the moldy homes and workplaces quietly making people sick, and convinced me of one thing: when mold is hidden and hard to find, a trained dog is the best answer there is.

Dogs On Mold exists so you don't have to lose what I lost along my journey. If you're sick and no one can explain why — or you just want to know what's really in your walls — this is for you.
